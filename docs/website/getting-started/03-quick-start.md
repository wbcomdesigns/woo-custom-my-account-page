# Quick Start

This walkthrough adds one custom page, puts it in the menu and checks it on your store. It takes about five minutes.

## Add a page to the account menu

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Woo My Account > Endpoints**.
2. Click **Add endpoint**.
3. Type a name, for example `Support`, and click **Save** in the dialog.
4. The new item appears at the bottom of the list. Click the **Endpoint** badge (or the arrow beside it) on that item to open its settings.
5. In **Endpoint content**, write what customers should see. You can use text, HTML and shortcodes.
6. Click **Save Changes**.

The new item is not stored until you click **Save Changes**.

## Check it on your store

1. Log in as a customer and open the My Account page.
2. Find **Support** in the menu and click it.
3. Confirm your content shows.

## Change the order

1. Go back to the **Endpoints** tab.
2. Drag the item by its title to a new position.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

## Choose the layout

1. Open the **General** tab.
2. Set **Menu layout** to **Sidebar** or **Tab**.
3. If you use Sidebar, set **Sidebar position** to Left or Right.
4. Click **Save Changes**.
