# Reorder, Hide and Remove Items

All changes on this page are stored when you click **Save Changes** on the Endpoints tab.

## Reorder

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Woo My Account > Endpoints**.
2. Drag a row by its title to a new position.
3. Drop it between other rows, or under a group to place it inside the group.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

Customers see the same order as the list.

## Hide an item

Hiding keeps the item and its settings but removes it from the menu.

1. Click the eye icon on the row, or open the item and click **Hide from menu**.
2. Click **Save Changes**.

To show it again, click the eye icon or **Show in menu**, then save.

Hiding is the only option for default WooCommerce items. They cannot be removed.

## Remove an item

Removing deletes a custom endpoint, group or link, including its content.

1. Open the item.
2. Click **Remove**.
3. In the **Delete menu item** dialog, click **Delete**.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

Until you save, the removal is not final. Leave the page without saving and the item stays.

If you remove a group, its items move up to the main list.

## Rename a default item

1. Open the default item, for example Orders.
2. Change **Endpoint label**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Only the menu label changes. The slug stays the same unless you edit it.

## Hide the default endpoint with care

If you hide the item that is set as the default endpoint, customers who open My Account are still sent to its URL. If that item is a custom endpoint, the hidden page then shows the default dashboard content instead of your content. Pick another default endpoint in the General tab before you hide it.
